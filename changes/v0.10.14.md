# v0.10.14

## New

- The contract says which texts are decimals (`format: decimal`, in a
  `decimal` field and in the amount of `Money`) and the currency of an
  amount sent without one (`default`, in `Money`, when the app says its
  `currency`), so that a generator knows them.

## Fixed

- The API answered the times of records in whole seconds, while the database keeps
  microseconds: `created_at_max` with a record's own `created_at` left the
  record out, and a `PUT` of what was read changed its times. It answers the
  microseconds now. A time with none is written as before.
- A request with an `Idempotency-Key` that the database refused, a value
  taken or a related record gone, answers why, and not `500`.

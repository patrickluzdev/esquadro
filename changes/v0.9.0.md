# v0.9.0

## Breaking

- What every frontend shares moved to the new `ui` blueprint, which the
  spa requires (add `blueprint ui` to the app, or `esquadro add ui`):
  - labels, singulars and icons of entities, labels and formats of fields,
    relations and report columns, labels and colors of enum values are
    `@ui(...)`: `@admin(label "Products", icon package)` becomes
    `@ui(label "Products", icon package)`;
  - a frontend's own annotation (`@admin`) keeps what only it has
    (`resource`, `menu`, `group`, widgets) and may still say a `label` or
    `singular` of its own, over `@ui`'s; `icon`, `format` and `color` are
    `@ui`'s only.

## New

- **Queues in the admin panel**: a resource's `queue` puts the records
  of a filter beside its table as cards, one in focus, worked with the
  keyboard; a record an action takes out of the filter leaves, and the
  next comes. Actions gain `key` (on the record in focus or open), `patch`
  (fields changed with `If-Match`, after the fields of `ask`) and `copy`;
  `run` is no longer required.

- **Redirects on a site**: `@site(redirect, by code, to target_url)`
  makes `/<code>` send to the record's address, with `BeforeRedirect`
  (where a visit goes, or `ErrGone` for the site's page of what ended) and
  `Visited` (what is kept of it: a `Visit` with `Bot`, a daily anonymous
  `Visitor`, `Referrer`) in the entity's rules: a link shortener from the
  model, without a blueprint of its own. Every unique field gets
  `Read<Entity>By<Field>`.

- **Integrations**: `blueprint integration <name> { url, rate_limit,
  retries, timeout, key, secret }`, a client of an outside API: retries
  that respect `Retry-After`, a rate limit, a timeout, `<NAME>_URL`,
  `<NAME>_KEY` and `<NAME>_SECRET` from the environment and never in
  errors, a span per call, the client in every module's `Deps`, a fake API
  (`<name>test`) for tests and `<app> <name> check`. Yours, `<name>.go`:
  how a request is authenticated and the API's calls.
- **Blueprints with instances** may declare `env INSTANCE_X`, which is
  `<NAME>_X` for each instance; their slots see `.Instance`.

- **Importing**: `@store(import)` and `@store(import_by field)`: records
  in a batch from a CSV in the admin panel (columns matched and
  remembered, values read in the panel's language, a preview with what is
  wrong) or by `POST /api/v1/<entities>/import`; all or none, each row
  through the rules of a create, and `import_by` replaces by a unique
  field instead of duplicating. Problems' `errors` may carry a `row`.

- **Settings**: `@store(single)`, an entity with one record only, always
  there with its defaults: `Read<Entity>(ctx)` and `Update`/`Patch`
  without an id, `GET`/`PUT`/`PATCH /api/v1/<entity>` (`POST` and
  `DELETE` answer 405), the TS client's `get`/`update`/`patch`, and a spa
  resource that opens on its form. A spa resource may no longer be named
  as one of the panel's own pages (`settings`, `audit`...).

- **Unique jobs**: `@jobs(unique)`, `unique_by [args]`, `unique_for "24h"`,
  `unique_while queued`: enqueued again, a job is not enqueued twice.

- **Business rules**: `internal/<module>/<entity>_rules.go`, yours, with
  `BeforeCreate`, `BeforeUpdate`, `BeforeDelete` and their `After`: every
  change runs them, in its transaction; `Broken` refuses with your code
  and text (422), `m.Lock` keeps what a rule counts right. Every write of
  the module's use cases now runs in a transaction.

- **Sites rendered on the server** (`blueprint ssr <name>`): each entity
  marked `@<name>(page)` gets a list (search, filters, orders, pages) and
  a page per record, with titles, Open Graph, structured data, a sitemap
  and robots.txt, read through the model's rules; the visitor signs in,
  up and out with the site's realm. The app's binary renders it (templ,
  htmx 4 and Tailwind).
- A request to a realm's identity routes reads only that realm's session:
  someone signed in to the panel signs up to the site in the same browser.

- The frontends' options are checked alike, and together: two frontends
  answering at the same place, or one under the app's own routes (`/api`,
  `/identity`, `/healthz`...), is an error; so are a domain or a path that
  are not one.
- Every frontend shares the same words for signing in, the account,
  errors, empty lists and fields, and the same theme from the same options.

## How to migrate

1. `esquadro add ui`.
2. In the `.esq`, move `label`, `singular`, `icon` (entities), `label`,
   `format` (fields, relations, report columns) and `label`, `color` (enum
   values) from `@<frontend>(...)` to `@ui(...)`; `esquadro check` says
   where a key is no longer the frontend's.
3. `esquadro upgrade`.

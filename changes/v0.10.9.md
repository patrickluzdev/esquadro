# v0.10.9

## New

- A site's record ends its address with a key of its own, after the slug
  of its title: `@site(page, key code)` makes `/offers/thermal-bottle-k7Qp2`,
  found by the key. A wrong slug and the old addresses by id
  (`/offers/<id>/<slug>`, `/offers/<id>`) move to it (`301`); a record
  without a key keeps its address by id. The project fills the key, in
  the entity's rules.
- A list gets a page per value of a filter: `@site(browse)` on a related
  record the list filters by makes `/offers/beauty`, and on an enum,
  `/offers/<segment>/<value>` (`path` names the segment). Each with its
  title, description, canonical address and breadcrumb, in the sitemap;
  the list filtered by that value's parameter alone points at it as its
  canonical address.
- A record's page is described by its title and price when it has no
  description; a product's structured data says its description, its
  picture, whether it is in stock (`@site(available [ACTIVE])` on an
  enum) and who sells it (`seller`); Open Graph calls it a `product`. A
  record's page and a filter's page have a `BreadcrumbList`, the steps
  they show (`@Breadcrumbs(v.Crumbs)`).
- A site answers `/llms.txt`, for AI agents: its name, its `description`
  (a new option, the first page's description too), and a section per
  list with its pages and its 50 latest records, those everyone may read.

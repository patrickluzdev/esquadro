# v0.10.12

## Fixed

- A password refused says why in the frontends, instead of "Review the
  highlighted fields": too short or too long (with the realm's minimum or
  maximum), too common, or found in a data breach. The panel's new password
  page and its settings' password card, and a site's sign-up and new
  password pages, also show the minimum under the field before it is sent.
  The settings' password card showed nothing at all when the new password
  was refused: the API names its field `password`.
- An email that is not one, on signing in, says so instead of "Review the
  highlighted fields".

## New

- A field refused `too_short` or `too_long` may carry `limit`, the length to
  reach or not pass: a password refused has the realm's minimum or maximum
  (`{"field": "password", "code": "too_short", "limit": 12}`). It is in the
  contract's `FieldProblem` and the TypeScript client's, and the identity
  routes that set a password say their codes and their `new_password`'s
  length.
- `passwordPolicy` in a panel's `src/esquadro/config.ts`: the realm's
  minimum and maximum.

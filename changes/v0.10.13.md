# v0.10.13

## New

- `Narrow` in an entity's policy (`<entity>_policy.go`, yours) narrows the
  API's list of its records to those the request may see, in the query, so
  that pages, totals and cursors hold only those: offers everyone may read,
  but only the approved ones unless a user signed in. The model's narrows
  none (`<entity>_policy_gen.go`); it has the last word over the request's
  own filters. Write `Read` to agree, for a record asked by its id. The
  sites do not ask it: what a site shows is its `shown.go`'s.
- `TestAPIAccess` expects a record in a list only when the policy's
  `Narrow` keeps it and its `Read` lets it in.

## Fixed

- An entity's list in the API answered every record its query read, even
  one the policy's `Read` refuses, which its `GET` by id answers `404`; it
  now leaves those out, with a page then shorter and a total that still
  counts them. So does a many-to-many relation's list
  (`GET /<plural>/{id}/<relation>`), by the other entity's `Read`. A model
  whose `list` lets in more than its `read` lists, to those `read` does not
  let in, nothing now.
- A panel calls a record by its title as people read it: an option's label
  ("WhatsApp channel"), not its value (`WHATSAPP_CHANNEL`), on the record's
  page, in the queue's cards and in the breadcrumb.
- A long value on a record's page (a link, an id) wraps inside its card
  instead of running past it.

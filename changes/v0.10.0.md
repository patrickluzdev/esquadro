# v0.10.0

## New

- **Images drawn by the app**: `blueprint images` generates
  `internal/platform/images`, a kit to draw images on the server in pure
  Go (`golang.org/x/image`): a canvas with a theme's colors and fonts,
  rectangles with round corners, text that wraps, shrinks and ends in an
  ellipsis, pictures contained or covering a box, PNG, and a cache in the
  storage blueprint's store. `@site(page, og_image)` gives each record's
  page an Open Graph image, `/<path>/<id>/og.png` (1200x630), in the
  site's brand (its colors, its TrueType or OpenType fonts, its logo):
  drawn by `<entity>_og.go`, the project's, written once; kept in the
  store and drawn again when the record, the drawing's version or the
  brand changes; served with an `ETag`, and the brand's `og_image` when
  it cannot be drawn. The record's page points its `og:image` at it,
  with its size. `esquadro check` says when `og_image` lacks the
  blueprint, and warns of a brand font the images cannot read (WOFF2).
  The storage store also keeps files of the app's own, by key
  (`Object`, `PutObject`, `DeleteObjects`), and reads a record's file
  (`Read`).

- **A site with a brand**: `brand "brand"` on an ssr site names the
  project's folder of its brand, with a `theme.json` (colors by role and
  its own, fonts, text roles of the project's names, radii, the height of
  controls, flat or raised buttons, motion, the pictures of the states,
  the logo, icons, default Open Graph picture and theme color) and the
  files it names. The app embeds the folder and serves it at `/brand/`,
  its icons at the root too; every page links the favicon, the manifest
  and the theme color and preloads the fonts; `theme.gen.css` writes the
  brand's tokens, fonts, `.t-<name>` classes and the kit's pieces
  (`.btn`, `.field`, `.chip`, `.card`, `.notice`, `.progress`); the error
  pages and empty lists show the brand's pictures, and the kit has
  `@Logo()` and `@StatePicture(...)`. `esquadro check` checks the brand.

- **Emails in the brand**: `brand "brand"` on the mail blueprint names
  the same folder a site may: every email's layout takes its colors, the
  names of its fonts (then the system's) and its logo, a PNG, JPEG or GIF
  (`identity.logo_email` in `theme.json` when the logo is an SVG), sent
  inside each email by SMTP and Resend alike, with the app's name in its
  place without one. A footer writes the app's name, why the reader got
  the email (in mail's `locale`, `en` or `pt_br`; a template's own `why`
  replaces it) and the `address` option, in the HTML and in the text,
  which `_layout.txt` now wraps. `{{button .Link "..."}}` writes a link as
  a button in the brand's accent, and security's emails use it. Without a
  brand, emails keep their neutral look. `_layout.html` and `_layout.txt`
  stay yours: one untouched takes the new layout on `esquadro gen`, one
  you changed has it merged in.

- **Emails in the app's language**: esquadro writes its emails (welcome
  and security's: their subjects, texts and HTML) and their dates in mail's
  `locale`, `en` or `pt_br`, by default the first frontend's. A copy the
  project never changed follows the locale on `esquadro gen`; one it
  changed has the new text merged in, with conflicts on the lines both
  changed. `mail test` sends its email in the layout, and `mail.Wrap`
  puts a message of your code's in it too.

- **A page on the way of a redirect**: `@site(redirect, by code, to
  target_url, wait 3)` shows a person a page in the site's layout that says
  where they are going (the destination's host), counts the seconds down
  (1 to 10) with a bar and goes on by itself, with a link to go at once;
  bots, a link's preview among them, still get the `302`. The page is the
  project's, `<entity>_redirect.templ`, written once; `Visited` runs when it
  is shown.

- **Copying an image in an action**: `copyImage: (r) => r.image_url` on an
  action of the admin panel copies the image as an image, to paste into
  a chat (as PNG, drawn on a canvas when it is another type), and says
  *Image copied*; a browser that cannot copy images offers to download it.
  It runs after `copy` and before `patch` and `run`, and is not a bulk
  action.

- **Value history**: `@store(history)` on an `int`, `int64`, `decimal` or
  `money` field keeps every value it takes, with when, in a table of its
  own (`offer_price_history`), added in the transaction of the create or
  the change that sets it; the module reads it as points
  (`OfferPriceHistory(ctx, id, since)`) or as a low and a high
  (`OfferPriceRange`, for "the lowest in 90 days"), the API at
  `GET /api/v1/offers/{id}/price-history`, the TS client as
  `api.offers.priceHistory(id, { since })`, and the admin panel draws it
  on the record's page.

- **Icons by name**: the admin panel draws its icons from one set,
  `src/esquadro/icons.gen.ts` (the kit's and those the model names, Lucide's
  by default), and `src/icons.tsx`, yours, gives any of them in their place,
  wherever it is drawn: `{ Trash2: MyBin }`. The panel bundles only the
  icons of the set, no longer all of Lucide's. A site draws its kit's
  icons with `@Icon("share-2")`, Lucide's in `icons_gen.go`, and `icons.go`,
  yours, gives its own. A file of yours that imports `lucide-react` itself
  still builds, and draws Lucide's.

## Fixed

- A site's assets (`site.css`, `kit.js`, htmx) are linked with a hash of
  their content and kept by browsers for good at that address, a few
  minutes at any other: after a deploy, visitors get the new styles and
  scripts at once, where before they could keep the old ones for an hour.
- The admin panel's components say in its language what they said in
  English: closing a dialog or a sheet, loading, the menu's toggle and
  title, the path and the pages (`close`, `toggleMenu`, `menuHint`,
  `more`, `morePages`, `previousPage`, `nextPage`, `path`, `pages` among
  the shared words); a test keeps the kits from writing words outside
  them.

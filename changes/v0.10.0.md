# v0.10.0

## New

- **A site with a brand**: `brand "brand"` on an ssr site names the
  project's folder of its brand, with a `theme.json` (colors by role and
  its own, fonts, text roles, radii, the height of controls, flat or
  raised buttons, motion, the pictures of the states, the logo, icons,
  default Open Graph picture and theme color) and the files it names. The
  app embeds the folder and serves it at `/brand/`, its icons at the root
  too; every page links the favicon, the manifest and the theme color and
  preloads the fonts; `theme.gen.css` writes the brand's tokens, fonts,
  `.t-<role>` classes and the kit's pieces (`.btn`, `.field`, `.chip`,
  `.card`, `.notice`, `.progress`, `.price`, `.photo`); the error pages
  and empty lists show the brand's pictures; the kit has `@Logo()`,
  `@Price(...)`, `@Photo(...)` and `@StatePicture(...)`. `esquadro check`
  checks the brand.

- **A page on the way of a redirect**: `@site(redirect, by code, to
  target_url, wait 3)` shows a person a page in the site's layout that says
  where they are going (the destination's host), counts the seconds down
  (1 to 10) with a bar and goes on by itself, with a link to go at once;
  bots, a link's preview among them, still get the `302`. The page is the
  project's, `<entity>_redirect.templ`, written once; `Visited` runs when it
  is shown.

- **Copying an image in an action**: `copyImage: (r) => r.image_url` on an
  action of the admin panel copies the image as an image, to paste into
  WhatsApp (as PNG, drawn on a canvas when it is another type), and says
  *Image copied*; a browser that cannot copy images offers to download it.
  It runs after `copy` and before `patch` and `run`, and is not a bulk
  action.

- **Value history**: `@store(history)` on an `int`, `int64`, `decimal` or
  `money` field keeps every value it takes, with when, in a table of its
  own (`offer_price_history`), added in the transaction of the create or
  the change that sets it; the module reads it as points
  (`OfferPriceHistory(ctx, id, since)`) or as a low and a high
  (`OfferPriceRange`, for "the lowest in 90 days"), the API at
  `GET /api/v1/offers/{id}/price-history`, the TS client as
  `api.offers.priceHistory(id, { since })`, and the admin panel draws it
  on the record's page.

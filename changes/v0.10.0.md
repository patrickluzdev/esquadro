# v0.10.0

## New

- **A site with a brand**: `brand "brand"` on an ssr site names the
  project's folder of its brand, with a `theme.json` (colors by role and
  its own, fonts, text roles, radii, the height of controls, flat or
  raised buttons, motion, the pictures of the states, the logo, icons,
  default Open Graph picture and theme color) and the files it names. The
  app embeds the folder and serves it at `/brand/`, its icons at the root
  too; every page links the favicon, the manifest and the theme color and
  preloads the fonts; `theme.gen.css` writes the brand's tokens, fonts,
  `.t-<role>` classes and the kit's pieces (`.btn`, `.field`, `.chip`,
  `.card`, `.notice`, `.progress`, `.price`, `.photo`); the error pages
  and empty lists show the brand's pictures; the kit has `@Logo()`,
  `@Price(...)`, `@Photo(...)` and `@StatePicture(...)`. `esquadro check`
  checks the brand.

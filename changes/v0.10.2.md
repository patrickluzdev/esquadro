# v0.10.2

## Breaking

- The app's address comes from `PUBLIC_URL`, in each environment, where it
  was the security blueprint's `public_url` option in all of them: set it
  in staging and production (`https://shop.com`), or the app does not
  start there; in development it is still `public_url`. Before, every link
  of an email (password, invitation, confirmation) and the providers'
  callbacks pointed at `http://localhost:8080` outside development unless
  the option was changed, and then at production from development too.

## New

- The team's page of a panel sends the confirmation of an email again,
  for an account whose email is not confirmed (Send the confirmation again,
  Reenviar confirmação de e-mail), from the new route
  `POST <prefix>/<realm>/accounts/{id}/verify-email`, for those who
  administer the realm; the link opens the panel's `/confirm-email?verify=1`.
- An invitation or an email's confirmation is sent again no sooner than 5
  minutes after the last one, nor more than 5 times a day per account
  (`resend_minutes`, `resend_per_day` of the security blueprint), counted
  in the new table `security_email_sends`: sooner, `429 too_soon` with
  `Retry-After`, and the account's `resend_at` says when, which the team's
  page shows on the action it disables. It holds for `.../invite`, and,
  silently, for `verify-email/send`.
- A role is called by its word, `role_<name>`, in a panel: its team's page,
  filters and dialogs, the service keys and the account's menu. The common
  roles have words in en and pt_br (`admin` is Administração); the project's
  own are named in `src/messages.ts`, or show as their name, capitalized.
- A frontend on a domain of its own answers on its alias too,
  `<name>.localhost` on the app's port (`http://site.localhost:8080/`,
  `http://admin.localhost:8080/` for a built panel), with the app's own
  paths (`/api`, `/identity`) reaching the app there: in development,
  nothing reached it before. On the alias, a site's canonical links, Open
  Graph and sitemap are on the alias. `web.Handle` and `web.Mount` take the
  frontend's name.

## Fixed

- The links of emails and of `security create`, `invite` and `reset` to a
  frontend on a domain of its own open that domain
  (`https://admin.shop.com/reset-password`, a site's
  `https://shop.com/nova-senha`), where they opened `/` or the frontend's
  path on `PUBLIC_URL`, which does not serve it. The panel sends its pages
  (the password's, the email's confirmation, an invitation's, where a
  provider sends back to) as addresses on its domain, a site its
  password's and, now, its sign-up's confirmation; the frontends' domains
  are allowed redirects without `redirect_urls`. In development those
  links open where the frontend is: a panel's where `task dev` serves it
  (`http://localhost:5173/reset-password`), a site's on its alias.
- A site on the app's domain sends its forgotten password's page as a path,
  which the app allows, where it sent an absolute address, which it
  refused unless `redirect_urls` held it.
- A panel on a domain of its own the app has not built says `task dev`
  serves it at `http://localhost:5173/`, where it said under its name's
  path.

- A count of one reads in the singular in a panel and a site: "1 conta",
  "1 dia", "1 encontrado", where it read "1 contas"; a word with a
  `{count}` has a form for one, `<key>_one`, which `t` and `T` take.
- The team's page says in the panel's language why an action was refused
  (one's own account, the last administrator, an email sent too recently),
  where it said only that there was a conflict.
- The security migrations apply one that comes with an option turned on
  later (the service keys') after newer ones.

- A record created by the API without a number or a yes/no field that has
  a default (`stock int default(3)`, `active bool default(true)`,
  `weight decimal default(1.5)`) takes that default, where it took 0 or
  false: refused when the field had a minimum above 0, and stored with
  the wrong value otherwise.
- `security create` and `security invite` send the link to the page of the
  realm's frontend that sets a password (`/admin/reset-password`, a site's
  `/nova-senha`), where they sent it to `/`, which has none; `-redirect`
  still chooses another.
- A panel the app has not built says, besides `task web:build`, where
  `task dev` serves it in development (`http://localhost:5173/admin/`).
- `security reset <realm> <email>` prints a new link that sets the password
  of an account that exists.
- In development, a link of an email or of `security create`/`reset` to a
  page of a panel opens where `task dev` serves it
  (`http://localhost:5173/admin/...`), where it opened the app's port,
  which serves no panel until it is built.

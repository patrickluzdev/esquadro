# v0.10.7

## New

- A card of a panel's form shows what you make of the form's values as
  they are typed: `sections: [{ title: "Preview", side: true, render:
  (values) => <Preview offer={values} /> }]`, under the card's fields or
  in place of them (`fields` may be left out). A preview of a message, a
  post or a card beside the fields that make it.

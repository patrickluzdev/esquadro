# v0.10.10

## New

- What a site shows is narrowed by `shown.go`, yours: `listed<Entities>`
  narrows the query of every list of the site (its list and its pages by
  a filter, the first page, the search, the sitemap and llms.txt), and
  `shown<Entity>` decides whether a record's page is there, beyond what
  `@security` lets everyone read. A page by a filter stays a page to
  index, with no parameter in its address to narrow it.
